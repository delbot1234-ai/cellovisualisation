Privacy Policy

Cello Bow Motion Explorer · Effective 23 September 2026

Cello Bow Motion Explorer runs entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of its own that receives your data.

Microphone

If you start the tuner, the app uses your microphone to detect the pitch you are playing. Audio is analysed in real time on your device and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere. The microphone is only active while the tuner is listening, and you can stop it at any time.

Bluetooth

If you connect a motion sensor (such as a WitMotion wrist sensor), the app uses Bluetooth only to receive motion data from the device you choose. That data is displayed and used on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

Files and session logs

Sensor recordings you load are read locally and never uploaded. The practice session log exists only on your device; it leaves your device only if you choose to download it as a CSV file.

Offline storage

The app stores a copy of its own program files on your device so it can work offline. It does not store personal information.

Hosting

The app's files are served from GitHub Pages. When your device downloads them, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. On Android, the app runs inside Google Chrome, whose handling of browsing data is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. For questions, contact the developer using the email address listed on the app's Google Play store page.

What am I looking at? (the physics, briefly)

For a cello string to vibrate cleanly (in "Helmholtz motion"), the bow force has to sit within a range that depends on bow speed and how far from the bridge you bow (the sounding point). Too little force and the bow slips too easily — you get a thin, breathy surface sound / whistle. Too much force and the string sticks and slips multiple times per cycle — a harsh crunch. This relationship is classically summarized by the Schelleng diagram: a wedge-shaped region of usable force that widens with bow speed and narrows as you bow further from the bridge.

This app uses a simplified, pedagogically-tuned version of that relationship (not a precise acoustic simulation) to make the trends explorable and audible.
